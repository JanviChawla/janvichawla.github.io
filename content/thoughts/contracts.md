where to even begin...

we are all energies\
we are up in some place but it's not called heaven\
everyone starts at level 1\
at level 1, your energy signs a contract that you live a perfect live
* no pain
* no suffering
* everything is perfect and you are spoonfed

you go down as assume the life given this contract\
when you die naturally you come back up\
at level 2, things get marginally harder.

as you can imagine, at level 124912048, someone's life can indeed be relatively more difficult than someone at level 112323. That person may have a crackhead mother in africa with seven subkungs whiile you are a middle class immigrant to a part of america that works as a meritocracy and enables you to progress

2 questions
* what cards do you think you were dealt? on a scale of 1 to 10
* how do you think you did with the cards you were dealt? on a scale of 1 to 10

for context, the girl who grew up in africa with 7 other siblings eating lipstick tubes to stay nourished was a 1 and put herself at a 10 because she is now a rhodes scholar, attending premium universities with full rides. not many people who started out at her "1" could have maximized opportunities they way she did. I personally ranked myself at a 5 and put myself at 6/7 for how I've used my cards. I certainly think I could've done more. But this is enough

if you die an unnatural death, you have to relive the level. \
these contracts are specific. \
my contract may have laid out exactly what it is that happens:
* my schooling
* my job
* my relationship
* my injuries
* my major life events

the itinerary of my whole life has in some way been planned, and the energy that came out after signing this contract is well aware. they can watch, they can witness, but they cannot act through me. there is only one time i have heard a voice say *STOP*. apart from that, they are viewing through the lens of your eyes, your thoughts, your feelings. does it give you peace that there is indeed someone always watching? this energy is not God.

If you knew someone else was "along for the ride," would you behave differently? Try to make it more fun for them? Is this what happens when we pull ourselves back to stabilization from an escalating situation? When we have the opportunity right now to pretend nothing in the past had ever happened, why do we go on carrying these suitcases... why bother with the baggage at all? Familiarity? We know what's in the suitcase? We don't even like what's in the suitcase, so why bother? Leave it behind, throw it out.

My psychiatrist said that all the medicines do is arm me to become the person I want to be, that I do indeed have the choice of architecting my personality. Now that I am on medication, I have no excuse. If I want to be something, I can. I know how to put in the work, we all do. Once all excuses have been refuted, what more can we do? Face the music... shoulders back, smile on face, proceed. Nothing to lose... so why do we convince ourselves that there is...?
